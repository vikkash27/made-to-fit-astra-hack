# Parts discovery extension

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing Made to Fit studio; preserve its graphite/ivory/orange landing and warm-paper studio styling, fonts, slim borders, and viewer behavior.

Audience: small electronics hobbyists, including first-time builders.
Task: enter through Explore my components, review photographed parts and optional appearance references, describe a purpose/tools/experience/parts budget, compare grounded concepts, measure only used parts, develop and accept checked CAD, and print/assemble from a frozen revision guide.

First viewport: the discovery empty state names the user's parts, asks what would make the build useful, offers purpose suggestions, and presents optional context without forcing prior electronics expertise. Keep a direct return to parts.
Signature interaction: each mechanical assembly step can highlight its corresponding parts in the actual accepted 3D assembly with a display-only explode.

Truth: Rodin is paid and appearance-only; measured envelopes govern fit. Unsupported concepts cannot enter the rectangular enclosure helper. Hardware remains distinct from printable parts. Additional hardware and firmware are explicit. The downloadable Markdown guide and interactive steps use the same revision/specification snapshot. Wiring, mounting, apertures, retention, heat and slicing require separate review; no invented pinouts or print settings.

Scope: local additions inside existing Parts/Discover/Confirm/Export stages. No replacement visual world or standalone screen concept selection. No new rasters or approved image comp.

Verification: dedicated test API/data on 8002 and frontend 5174. Synthetic example inventory is used only for local verification; native CAD artifacts are real. Do not alter the other agent's servers on 8001/5173.
