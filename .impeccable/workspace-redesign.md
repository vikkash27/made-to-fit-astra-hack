# Made to Fit workspace redesign

Mode: Operate. User explicitly approved a white workspace with the existing orange accent. Replace the compressed document layout while preserving the typed backend and actual geometry. No generated image comp: the working photo and CAD are the design artifacts.

First viewport: a plain step heading, short task guidance, large parts photo and authentic cropped-part list. A single next-step action prioritises review over optional preferences. Orange marks actions/selection, green confirmed states, amber unresolved review. White is the ground of the builder and WebGL viewer; graphite remains the home scene.

Signature interactions: selecting a numbered photo region selects the matching part and review form. The Astra launcher follows every stage, opens a dock on broad displays and an accessible drawer on narrower displays, and retains its draft. Locked stages explain the dependency and link to its resolution. Visited steps preserve unsaved form inputs.

Shapes: 8px fields, 12–16px artifact surfaces, rounded action controls, soft tonal areas, borders for fields/dividers. Avoid stacked dashboard cards; lists, photos and models carry the information.

Home must explain: turn electronics parts into grounded project plans and custom measured 3D-printable enclosures. Two entry modes share review → choose → measure → build/check → explicitly accept → print/assemble.

User steering: remove provider and payment wording from the UI. Create detailed appearance files automatically only for reviewed photo parts. Stable operation IDs and existing-job detection prevent repeat or unknown submissions. Appearance never establishes measured fit. Keep backend provider identifiers internal.

Verification: desktop and phone evidence, browser functional journey, frontend tests/type-check/build, native CAD and accepted artifact checks in isolated test data. The assembly-guide chat owns BuildGuide, wiring components and backend typed additions; avoid overwriting them.
